# Sonec · Design system

The website and both apps share one look, taken from the Grupo Sonec logo: a black badge, brushed silver letters and a copper "S".

## Colors
| Role | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink / night | `#0d0d0f` | `#0d0d0f` | Header, hero, app headers, footer |
| Paper | `#f3f1ee` | `#121214` | Page background |
| Card | `#ffffff` | `#1a1a1d` | Cards, forms |
| Line | `#e2ded8` | `#2a2a2e` | Borders, dividers |
| Muted text | `#62605c` | `#a4a29d` | Secondary text |
| Copper (fill) | `#a24c1c` | `#a24c1c` | Buttons and selected states, white text on top (5.3:1) |
| Copper (light) | `#ec9259` | `#ec9259` | Accents on dark backgrounds only |
| Silver | `#b9bec5` | `#b9bec5` | Logo, fine details on dark |
| Success | `#2f7d4f` | `#62c48a` | Paid, done |
| Danger | `#b23a26` | `#ec7a5a` | Decline, over budget |

## Type
- **Barlow Semi Condensed 600/700:** headings (sentence case, line-height 1.05 to 1.1).
- **Barlow 400 to 700:** body, 16 to 17px, 1.55 line-height.
- **Montserrat 700:** only inside the logo wordmark.

## Shape and space
- Radius: 10px for buttons and inputs, 14px for cards, 999px for pills.
- Touch targets: 44px minimum. Inputs: 16px text so iPhone does not zoom.
- Content width: 1120px on the site, 1000px in the apps. Bands are full width.

## Components
Primary button (copper fill), secondary button (outlined), pill (status), KPI tile, card, timeline dot, phone frame (site only).

## Rules
- Real photos of Sonec work only. No stock photos, no invented reviews, numbers or prices.
- Every screen in Spanish and English.
- Contrast at least 4.5:1 for text (checked with axe).
